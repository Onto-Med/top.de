* **[SMITH](https://www.smith.care/en/)**: In the SMITH Consortium, a network of university and university medical partners is working on linking research and healthcare in a targeted and data protection-compliant manner.
* **[GeMTeX](https://www.medizininformatik-initiative.de/en/gemtex-automated-indexing-medical-texts-research)**: A cross-consortium project led by SMITH that processes and automatically indexes anonymized German medical texts from routine care to make unstructured patient records accessible for medical research.
* **[INTERPOLAR](https://www.smith.care/de/interpolar_mii/interpolar/)**: Cross-consortium projects aimed at enhancing medication safety and identifying health risks associated with polymedication.
* **[LIFE](https://www.life.uni-leipzig.de)**: Leipzig Research Center for Civilization Diseases / LIFE Management Cluster at Leipzig University, providing central infrastructure and management coordination across clinical research projects including SMITH and INTERPOLAR.
* **[ANNO](https://annosaxfdm.de)**: The ANthropological Notation Ontology (ANNO) allows the systematic and standardized classification of recovered bone finds into the skeletal system, the description of the skeletal pieces, and the definition of functions for deriving different phenotypes of humans in forensic and historical anthropology.
